# Water Pollution

Water Pollution is the introduction of harmful substances such as chemicals, waste, or bacteria into water bodies (rivers, lakes, oceans), making the water toxic or unsafe for humans and the environment.

It is defined as the alteration in physical, chemical, or biological characteristics of water, making it unsuitable for designated use in its natural state.

It occurs when pollutants are discharged directly or indirectly into water bodies without adequate treatment.

---

## Types of Water Pollutants

There are two main types of sources:

~ **Point Sources:**

Sources that discharge pollutants at specific locations through pipes, ditches, or sewers.

**Example:** Factories, Sewage treatment plants.

~ **Non-Point Sources:**

Sources that cannot be traced to a single site of discharge.

**Example:** Agricultural runoff (fertilizers from fields), Acid rain.

---

## Causes of Water Pollution

Following are the important causes of water pollution:

~ **Industrial Waste:**

Industries release toxic chemicals like lead, mercury, and sulphur into rivers and oceans.

This changes the color and temperature of the water and is hazardous to aquatic life.

~ **Sewage and Wastewater:**

Domestic sewage and wastewater contain harmful bacteria and chemicals.

When released into water bodies without treatment, they cause serious health issues.

~ **Agricultural Activities:**

Farmers use chemical fertilizers and pesticides to protect crops.

When it rains, these chemicals mix with water and flow into rivers (Runoff), causing pollution.

~ **Marine Dumping:**

Garbage like plastic, paper, and aluminum are deposited into the sea.

These take years to decompose and harm marine animals.

~ **Oil Spills:**

Accidental oil spills from ships tanker affect marine life badly.

Oil forms a thick layer on the water surface, blocking sunlight and oxygen.

~ **Thermal Pollution:**

Power plants release hot water into nearby rivers.

This sudden rise in temperature kills fish and other aquatic organisms.

---

## Effects of Water Pollution

The hazardous effects of water pollution include:

~ **Diseases (Human Health):**

Drinking polluted water causes water-borne diseases like Cholera, Typhoid, Diarrhea, and Jaundice.

Heavy metals like Mercury cause Minamata disease.

~ **Eutrophication:**

Excess nutrients (nitrogen/phosphorus) from fertilizers cause rapid growth of algae (Algal Bloom).

This depletes oxygen in the water, killing fish and other aquatic life.

~ **Destruction of Ecosystems:**

Water pollution destroys aquatic ecosystems.

Animals and plants that depend on water bodies die or lose their habitats.

~ **Bio-magnification:**

Toxic chemicals enter the food chain (e.g., small fish eat poison -> big fish eat small fish -> humans eat big fish).

The concentration of toxin increases at each level, severely affecting humans.

---

## Control Measures of Water Pollution

Following are the measures to control water pollution:

~ **Sewage Treatment Plants:**

Proper treatment of domestic sewage before discharging it into rivers is essential.

~ **Industrial Waste Treatment:**

Industries must treat their toxic waste (Effluent Treatment Plants) to remove harmful chemicals before releasing it.

~ **Minimize Use of Pesticides:**

Farmers should use organic manure instead of chemical fertilizers and pesticides.

~ **Strict Laws:**

The government should implement strict laws (like the Water Prevention and Control of Pollution Act) to punish polluters.

~ **Public Awareness:**

Educate people not to throw garbage or plastic into rivers and lakes.
