# Air Pollution

Air Pollution is the release of pollutants such as gases, particles, biological molecules, etc. into the air that is harmful to human health and the environment.

## Types of Air Pollutants

There are two types of air pollutants:

**I) Primary Pollutants:**

The pollutants that directly cause air pollution are known as primary pollutants.

**Example:** Sulphur-dioxide emitted from factories.

**II) Secondary Pollutants:**

The pollutants formed by the intermingling and reaction of primary pollutants are known as secondary pollutants.

**Example:** Smog (Smoke + Fog)

---

## Causes of Air Pollution

Following are the important causes of air pollution:

~ **Burning of Fossil Fuels:**

I) The combustion of fossil fuels emits a large amount of sulphur dioxide.

II) Incomplete combustion releases Carbon monoxide.

~ **Automobiles:**

I) Gases emitted from vehicles (jeeps, trucks, cars, buses) pollute the environment.

II) They are major sources of greenhouse gases.

~ **Agricultural Activities:**

I) Ammonia is a hazardous gas emitted during agricultural activities.

II) Insecticides, pesticides, and fertilizers emit harmful chemicals.

~ **Factories and Industries:**

Factories are the main source of carbon monoxide, organic compounds, and chemicals.

~ **Mining Activities:**

I) In the mining process, minerals are extracted using large equipment.

II) The dust and chemicals released deteriorate the health of workers.

~ **Domestic Sources:**

I) Household cleaning products and paints release toxic chemicals.

II) The smell from newly painted walls affects breathing.

## Effects of Air pollution

The hazardous effects of air pollution include:

~ **Diseases (Health Effects):**

It results in respiratory disorders and heart diseases.

Lung cancer cases have increased.

Children living near polluted areas are prone to pneumonia and asthma.

~ **Global Warming:**

Emission of greenhouse gases causes an imbalance in gaseous composition.

This leads to an increase in Earth's temperature (Global Warming), melting of glaciers, and rise in sea levels.

~ **Acid Rain:**

Burning fossil fuels releases Nitrogen oxides and Sulphur oxides.

These combine with water droplets to form acidic rain, which damages plants and animals.

~ **Ozone Layer Depletion:**

Release of Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) and halons destroys the ozone layer.

This allows harmful UV rays to enter, causing skin diseases and eye problems.

## Control Measures of Air Pollution

Following are the measures to control air pollution:

~ **Avoid Using Vehicles:**

Avoid using vehicles for shorter distances.

Prefer public transport to save energy and prevent pollution.

~ **Energy Conservation:**

A large number of fossil fuels are burnt to generate electricity.

Switch off electrical appliances when not in use.

Use energy-efficient devices like CFLs.

~ **Use of Clean Energy Resources:**

Use solar, wind, and geothermal energies to reduce pollution.

India has implemented these resources for a cleaner environment.
